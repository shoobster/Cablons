Ownership, not just advice.

Plenty of firms will send a consultant to write a strategy document. A fractional CISO engagement is different: we take ownership of the security programme, sit in your leadership meetings, answer to your board and your customers, and are measured on whether risk actually goes down.

  • Security strategy and a costed 12 to 24 month roadmap
  • Risk register ownership and quarterly board reporting
  • Customer security questionnaires and enterprise due diligence calls
  • Supplier risk management and contract security clauses
  • Incident response planning, tabletop exercises and first call when something goes wrong
  • Oversight of your MSP, SOC and other security suppliers
  • Security input to product, cloud and architecture decisions

Engagement options

Advisory
Two days a month. Strategy, risk and board reporting, with ad hoc calls in between.
Embedded
Four to six days a month. Everything in Advisory plus hands-on programme delivery and supplier management.
Project
Fixed-term cover for a specific outcome: a certification, an audit, an incident or a leadership gap while you recruit.

All options are monthly rolling with 30 days' notice. We will tell you if you have outgrown us.
