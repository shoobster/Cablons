Who we are

Cablons Cyber Limited is a UK registered consultancy based in West Yorkshire, working with clients on site across the North of England and beyond, and remotely anywhere in the UK. We are deliberately small, and every engagement is led by a senior consultant rather than handed down. Our consultants hold CISSP and ISO 27001 Lead Auditor certifications and have come to consultancy from the defending side: the founder spent a decade on the blue team at an ISP, running first-line security and then the network team, before fifteen years in penetration testing, ISO 27001 audit and implementation, and advisory work. We hire and partner with people who have the same mix of hands-on and governance experience.

How we think about security

Security is a business function. The point of a penetration test is not a long list of findings; it is a shorter list of things that would actually hurt, fixed. The point of ISO 27001 is not the certificate; it is a business that can show a customer, an insurer or a regulator how it manages risk, and keep doing so after the auditor leaves.

We scope tightly, write in plain English, rank by business impact rather than CVSS alone, and tell clients when they are about to spend money on something that will not reduce risk.
