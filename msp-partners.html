Talk about partnering

Why MSPs come to us

The clients you manage are being asked questions you did not sign up to answer. A larger customer wants ISO 27001. An insurer wants a penetration test from someone other than the firm that runs the network. A board wants a security strategy and a name against it. Hiring a CISO to serve a handful of clients does not add up, and sending the work to a large consultancy means they meet your client without you.

We fill that gap. Our consultants have run service desks and network teams, so we understand how an MSP works and where the margin is. We do the senior security work; you keep the client, the infrastructure and the remediation.

How the arrangement works

Most partners start with one client and one engagement, usually a fractional CISO retainer or a gap analysis, and grow from there. We can work under your brand with your engineers as the delivery team, or appear as a named partner on your proposals. Either way we agree in writing that we do not sell managed services, so there is no risk of us competing for the account.

Referral and resale terms are simple and agreed up front, and we are happy to join your sales calls when a client wants to meet the people doing the work.

What we bring

  • Fractional CISO engagements for your clients, with you as the technical partner
  • Independent penetration testing with the separation auditors and insurers want to see
  • ISO 27001, Cyber Essentials and sector readiness programmes your engineers help deliver
  • A second opinion on your own security posture and the questionnaires your clients send you
  • Supplier due diligence on the vendors you resell

A typical first engagement

Week one
Introduction call with you, then a scoping call with the client. Fixed proposal issued under whichever brand you prefer.
Weeks two to four
Gap analysis or first month of fractional CISO cover. Your engineers are copied on anything technical.
Month two onwards
Remediation handed to your team, with us owning the governance, the reporting and the conversation with the client's leadership.

Partners are generally UK MSPs with between 10 and 100 staff and clients in the 20 to 500 seat range.
