Compliance work we take on

ISO 27001 gap analysis

A structured review against ISO 27001:2022 and Annex A, producing a clause-by-clause gap report, a prioritised plan and a realistic estimate of the time and cost to certification.

ISO 27001 implementation

Risk assessment, Statement of Applicability, policy set, control implementation support and internal audit, taking you through to Stage 1 and Stage 2 with your chosen certification body.

Readiness assessments

Assessments against NIST CSF 2.0, PCI DSS v4, Cyber Essentials and sector frameworks, with a scored baseline and a roadmap your leadership can fund.

Policies and procedures

A policy set written for your organisation rather than downloaded from a template library, short enough that people read it and specific enough that auditors accept it.

Supplier and customer assurance

Help answering security questionnaires, running supplier due diligence, and building the evidence pack that shortens every future enterprise sales cycle.

Internal audit and ongoing support

Annual internal audit, management review facilitation and surveillance audit preparation on a light retainer, so certification does not lapse the year after you get it.

Controls that fit the business, not the other way round.

A management system that only exists to pass an audit will be ignored by week three. We start from how the business actually operates, keep documentation to what the standard requires and the business will use, and build evidence collection into existing processes rather than adding a parallel one.

Because our consultants audit as well as implement, we know where certification bodies push and where they do not. That keeps scope tight and avoids gold-plating controls nobody has asked for.

A certificate you can keep with a day a month of effort, not one that needs a consultant on site every quarter.
