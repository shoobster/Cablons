Clear scope, clear findings, clear next steps.

Every engagement runs the same way, whether it is a two-day test or a nine-month certification programme.

Scoping call

Thirty minutes to understand what you need evidence of, what is in scope and what is off limits. You get a fixed quote within two working days.

Delivery

Testing or assessment work runs to an agreed window, with a named contact and daily check-ins. Critical findings are raised the day they are found.

Reporting

A risk-ranked findings list with reproduction steps, business impact and practical remediation. Presented live, not just emailed.

Retest and support

Fixes are verified and the report updated so you have clean evidence for customers, auditors and insurers.
